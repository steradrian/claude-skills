# Storyboard

Concept: Called it waste - the factory's rubbish, cut and taped back together into the shoe.
Why this concept won over the other two: the product IS the collage. The style (torn scraps, tape,
ransom letters) is literally how the shoe is made, so the look proves the claim instead of
decorating it.
Hook line: "They called it WASTE."
Arc: accusation (waste) → evidence (the scraps) → transformation (scraps assemble into the shoe,
"We called it OFFCUT 01") → proof (four fast facts) → brand (chaos snaps into the logo).
