# Review rubric

The critic works through this list on every pass. The builder should too, before asking the critic.

## Every look

- **Story with the sound off:** does the contact sheet read as the direction's arc, in order?
- **Hook:** is there motion or a question in the first 1.5 s?
- **One bold moment:** is there exactly one, and does it land?
- **Text:** legible at phone size, inside the safe area, never glowing, fringed or clipped, and on screen long enough (see the pacing rules).
- **Edges:** anything important cut off at the frame edge, including during camera drift?
- **Energy:** do adjacent beats differ in speed and scale, or does it read as a slideshow?
- **Specificity:** could this be any product's film? If so, say "rethink".
- **Report flags:** verify each one on the frame and either confirm it or explain why it's intended.
- **Sound:** does the loudness arc match the plan? No clipping, and no dead air unless it's a planned silence.

## Photoreal

- Blacks are at 0 (check the report's darkest 1% column).
- No glossy surface is white from edge to edge unless intended. If one is, name the light.
- Macros have one clear point of focus, and the subject is recognisable.
- The product has a readable silhouette in every lit shot (rim light, reflection, or glow).
- The floor reflection is in frame on wide shots.
- Screen UI doesn't bloom, and its text is sharp.

## Stylized

- Every object casts a soft shadow onto the set.
- Palette discipline holds (background, hero, accent, ink), with no extra colours creeping in.
- Motion has springs and squash where physical, and is staggered, not synchronized.
- Objects in front of or behind the product create depth.
- Nothing moves without a reason.

## 2D motion

- The type scale holds: clear hierarchy between display, caption and UI.
- Text enters through masks or with purpose, not a generic fade-up.
- Depth cues (tinted shadows, lift, parallax) separate the layers.
- Transitions carry meaning and land on sound.
- The UI is real and specific, and each interaction lands before the next starts.
